LinkShield Draft Tester Opt-Out

If you are participating in the LinkShield draft testing programme and no longer wish to participate, you can request removal from the tester list.

To opt out: Send an email to nandinisinghal1204@gmail.com with the subject line LinkShield - Tester Opt-Out.

Please send the request from the Google account you used to join the draft testing programme so the correct tester account can be identified.

After receiving your request, the developer will remove the relevant account from the LinkShield draft tester list.
