1. What LinkShield does

LinkShield extracts links from the currently opened Gmail message and lets you request an analysis of an individual link. The analysis may use basic URL indicators and information returned by the URLhaus threat-intelligence service. LinkShield displays the resulting indicators and risk assessment for informational purposes.
